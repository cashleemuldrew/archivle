Privacy policy

Last updated October 19, 2026.

What Archivle stores

Archivle has no accounts and doesn't collect your name or email. Your guesses and stats (games played, wins, streaks) are saved in your own browser's local storage so they're there when you come back. They never leave your device. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

Outside services

Loading the site connects your browser to these services, which can see your IP address and basic browser information:

  • Grailed serves the item photos.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's typefaces.
  • The web host (GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages) serves the site's files and may keep standard server logs.

Advertising

Archivle may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet. You can turn off personalized advertising in Google's Ad Settings, and read how Google uses data in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Sponsor and affiliate links

Some links may be sponsored or affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, the other site may share a commission with Archivle. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Contact
